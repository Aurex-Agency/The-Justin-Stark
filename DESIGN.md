# Design System: The Justin Stark

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Editorial Lux Portrait"**

This world is a modern editorial-lux magazine feature on a near-black ground: Justin's real photography blended full-bleed into the dark, oversized Playfair Display headlines with italic gold turns, and authentic gold rationed to 2px bars, 1px hairlines, small caps, and one gold button per view. It refuses template flatness — no icon-card grids, no boxed sections, no gradient text; glow is reserved for gold-button hover (a user-requested, earned exception). Sections are open compositions laid directly on the page; structure is drawn with hairline rules and asymmetric 5/7 grids, not with cards. A faint fixed film grain (inline SVG turbulence at 2.8% opacity) keeps the black from reading as flat pixels.

Photography leads. The first viewport is a split hero: the portrait bleeds in from the right behind a hairline offset frame, scrimmed into the black on every edge; copy owns the left 60%. Everywhere else the same discipline holds — images are slightly desaturated, overlaid with near-black and navy gradients, and never pasted on as bright rectangles. Motion is confident and quiet: the hero photo breathes in, copy rises on a stagger, credentials glide in a slow 36s marquee (paused on hover/focus, static on touch and narrow screens), and below-the-fold content fades up via IntersectionObserver. One ease drives everything, and the page is fully visible without JS or with reduced motion.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Photo-forward: real photography blended into near-black with scrims, desaturation, and hairline offset frames
- Oversized Playfair 900 headlines (hero up to 5.9rem, lh 1.08, -0.015em) ending in italic-600 gold turns; Inter 400–700 as the functional voice
- Metallic gold accent (#b98a2f core / #d4af37 bright, #f2dd85 highlight to #96701e shadow) rendered as polished metal on buttons and 2px foil bars; hairlines and small caps stay flat gold
- Editorial full-width rows and asymmetric 5/7 grids instead of card grids; hairline rules draw all structure
- A scrolling credential marquee as the trust strip — uppercase Playfair with gold diamond separators
- One easing everywhere: `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`; motion opts in via JS and honors `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Colors

A near-black ground warmed by one metal — authentic gold in two temperatures — over a navy undertone that lives only inside gradients.

### Primary
- **Gold** (`#b98a2f`): the standing gold — small-caps meta lines and field labels, marquee diamond separators, focus outlines, selection background, and the darker stop of every gold gradient.
- **Gold Light** (`#d4af37`): the brighter gold for shine — italic `<em>` turns inside headings, engraved links, default link color, hover states on nav/wordmark/links, and the brighter stop of the gradients. The two golds meet only on surfaces: the button face (`linear-gradient(180deg, #d4af37, #b98a2f)`) and the 2px accent bars (`linear-gradient(90deg, #d4af37, #b98a2f)`) — never on text.
- **Gold Rule** (`rgba(185,138,47,.32)`) and **Gold Rule Dim** (`rgba(185,138,47,.14)`): gold diluted into hairlines. Rule (.32) draws emphatic strokes — the portrait's offset frame, hairline-button borders, dashed placeholder underlines, meta-line middots. Rule Dim (.14) draws quiet structure — the hero photo's offset frame, marquee band and speaking band top/bottom rules, contact-band top rule, signature rule, scrolled header border.

### Neutral
- **Near-Black** (`#08090c`): the page ground, the color every photo scrim resolves to, the scrolled header (`rgba(8,9,12,.9)` + 14px blur), and the mobile menu (.98).
- **Panel** (`#0d0f15`): the only solid raised surface — the hover fill of a path row, the `<select>` dropdown, and the contact band's gradient start.
- **Panel Raised** (`#12151d`): defined as a token but currently unused by any component; reserve it for a surface one step above Panel.
- **Navy Undertone** (`#0b1a33`): never a flat fill — it tints. It appears only inside gradients and washes: the hero's radial depth (at .5, centered 18% 30%), the hero-photo and speaking scrims, the marquee band wash, the portrait's corner tint, the contact band and footer fades.
- **Warm Ink** (`#f4f1ea`): headings, primary text, marquee items, mosaic captions, `<strong>` inside grey copy, wordmark, hover state of gold links.
- **Letterpress Grey** (`#a7a9b4`): supporting copy, ledes, path-list rows, portrait caption, nav links at rest, placeholder slots, form placeholders (at .75 alpha).
- **Ink Hairline** (`rgba(244,241,234,.07)`): the neutral structural hairline — path-row separators, reach-list rules, footer legal rule. **Ink Underline** (`rgba(244,241,234,.22)`): the resting underline of form fields.
- **Pressed Ink** (`#14100a`): dark text on gold surfaces — gold buttons, the skip link, text selection.

### Named Rules
**The Gold Budget Rule.** Gold is spent on 2px bars, 1px hairlines, small caps, italic turns, diamond separators, and at most one gold-filled button per view. It is never a background wash, never a text gradient, never a glow.

**The Two Golds Rule.** `#b98a2f` is standing gold (labels, separators, outlines, dark gradient stop); `#d4af37` is gold catching light (emphasis, links, hovers, bright gradient stop). Hover resolves gold toward ink (`#f4f1ea`), not toward brighter gold.

**The Undertone Rule.** Navy `#0b1a33` appears only inside gradients, scrims, and tints — never as a flat panel color, border, or text color.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Playfair Display, variable 700–900 upright + a separate italic-600 face (Georgia, 'Times New Roman' fallback) — self-hosted woff2 in `assets/fonts/`, `font-display: swap`, preloaded.
**Body Font:** Inter, variable 400–700 (system-UI fallback) — self-hosted, preloaded.

**Character:** Editorial and oversized. Playfair at 900 runs tight (line-height 1.08, -0.015em, `text-wrap: balance`) and big — the hero headline reaches 5.9rem. Inter stays functional and quiet. Ceremonial = Playfair; operational = Inter; the marquee is the one place Playfair goes uppercase.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, `clamp(3rem, 6.8vw, 5.9rem)`, 1.08): hero headline only; max-width 13ch; drops to `clamp(2.6rem, 12vw, 3.4rem)` at ≤520px.
- **Headline** (900, per-section clamps, 1.08): section h2s at graded scales — How-I-Help `clamp(2.2rem, 4.6vw, 3.6rem)`, speaking `clamp(2.4rem, 5vw, 4rem)`, contact `clamp(2.2rem, 4.6vw, 3.4rem)`, about `clamp(2.1rem, 4.2vw, 3.2rem)`. Every headline ends with an italic turn: the final phrase in `<em>` — Playfair italic 600, Gold Light.
- **Title** (700, `clamp(1.6rem, 2.6vw, 2.1rem)`): path-row headings; mobile-menu links at 1.7rem.
- **Marquee** (Playfair 700, 1.02rem, 0.14em, uppercase, Warm Ink): the credential voice; steps down to a static 0.84rem wrapped row at ≤900px / pointer:coarse.
- **Flourish** (Playfair italic 600, Gold Light): path promises (1.15rem), the signature (1.55rem), the form thank-you line (1.9rem). Grey italic at .85rem for quiet asides: path notes, form reassurance, reach note.
- **Body** (Inter 400, 1.0625rem, 1.7): paragraphs capped at 68ch; hero sub `clamp(1.02rem, 1.4vw, 1.18rem)` at 52ch; section heads and ledes inside 64ch. `<strong>` inside grey copy = Warm Ink at 600 — the in-paragraph emphasis system.
- **Label** (Inter 600, 0.72–0.8rem, 0.16–0.22em, uppercase): small caps (.78rem/.22em, Gold), field and reach labels (.72rem/.2em), buttons (.8rem/.18em), nav (.8rem/.16em, weight 500), portrait caption (.78rem/.2em, grey), mosaic captions (.74rem/.2em, ink), wordmark (Playfair 700, 1.12rem/.22em uppercase; .95rem in footer).

### Named Rules
**The No-Kicker Rule.** The small-caps voice is a voice for lines of content — the hero conference line, the speaking meta line, captions, field labels — with middot separators in Gold Rule when listed (separators hide and lines stack at ≤520px). It never sits above a heading as a kicker or eyebrow. Headings introduce themselves.

**The Italic Turn Rule.** Emphasis inside any Playfair heading is italic 600 in Gold Light — never bolder weight, never underline, never a second color family.

## Layout

Sections are open editorial compositions laid directly on the near-black ground — no cards, no boxed sections. Content lives in a `min(100% - 3rem, 1180px)` centered container. Vertical rhythm is 7.5rem: `.section` blocks carry `padding: 7.5rem 0 0`; the speaking and contact bands carry `margin-top: 7.5rem` plus their own interior padding (`clamp(7rem, 14vw, 11rem)` and `clamp(5rem, 9vw, 7.5rem)`).

The recurring spatial move is the asymmetric split: 5fr/7fr for path rows (copy/list), the about grid (portrait/letter), and the contact grid (intro/form); 7fr/5fr for the photo mosaic (tall figure spanning two rows). Grid gaps run `clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)` (mosaic `clamp(1rem, 2vw, 1.6rem)`; path rows `2rem 3.5rem`). Section heads cap at 64ch: headline, grey lede (1.3rem above), then a 74px × 2px gold bar 1.9rem below; content follows at 4rem. Path rows bleed 1.5rem past the container edge (`margin-inline: -1.5rem` with matching padding) so their hover fill runs wider than the text column.

The hero fills `100svh` as a split composition: copy (max-width 60%) on the left of the container, the photo absolutely positioned to the right at 47% viewport width, its scrims dissolving it into the ground on all sides, with a hairline offset frame inset 9% top/bottom, 12% left, 8% right. A navy radial (`ellipse 80% 55% at 18% 30%`, .5) gives the left side depth. Hero inner padding: 8rem top, 5rem bottom.

The fixed header is transparent over the hero and gains material past 24px of scroll: near-black at .9, 14px backdrop blur, Gold Rule Dim bottom border, soft black shadow. Smooth scroll with `scroll-padding-top: 5.5rem`.

**Responsive:** at ≤1100px the hero rebalances (copy 50%, photo 52%). At ≤900px the nav collapses to a hairline hamburger and full-screen menu; the hero stacks — photo goes 100% wide behind the copy, offset frame removed, scrim rotates vertical (`rgba(8,9,12,.62) → .45 → .88 → near-black` top-to-bottom), content bottom-aligned (`align-items: flex-end`, padding 9rem/4.5rem); path rows, about, mosaic, and contact collapse to one column; path lists drop to one text column; the portrait caps at 460px centered; the marquee goes static. At ≤520px field rows stack, hero CTAs go full-width, meta lines stack with separators hidden, and the mosaic's tall figure drops to 340px min-height. At ≤360px the wordmark tightens.

**The Row, Not Card Rule.** Repeatable content is an editorial row divided by ink hairlines (path rows, reach list), never a grid of boxed cards. Hover may fill a row with Panel; it never adds a border or shadow.

## Elevation & Depth

Essentially flat: depth comes from photography blended into the dark, navy gradient washes, and hairline rules — not from floating panels. Solid surface changes are rare (a path row filling with Panel `#0d0f15` on hover; the contact band's Panel-to-navy gradient). Shadows exist in exactly three places, always neutral black, soft, cast downward: the about portrait (`0 30px 60px -32px rgba(0,0,0,.9)`), gold buttons on hover-lift (`0 14px 28px -14px rgba(0,0,0,.75)`), and the scrolled header (`0 10px 30px -18px rgba(0,0,0,.8)`). Overlays on photography use the same neutral family: caption gradients resolve to `rgba(8,9,12,.88)`, hero and speaking scrims layer `rgba(8,9,12,.55–.97)` with navy tints. The fixed grain overlay (opacity .028, z-index 60, pointer-events none) textures everything.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Portrait weight** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -32px rgba(0,0,0,.9)`): under the about portrait only — the heaviest shadow in the system.
- **Hover lift** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 28px -14px rgba(0,0,0,.75)` with `translateY(-2px)`): gold button hover.
- **Header float** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -18px rgba(0,0,0,.8)`): scrolled fixed header only.

### Named Rules
**The Black Shadow Rule.** Every shadow and photo overlay is neutral black (or the near-black/navy scrim family) at low spread, cast downward. No colored glows, no gold halos — gold shines by contrast, not by bloom.

## Shapes

Square. Corners are 0 on every surface — photos, frames, rows, fields, bands. The only radius is a 2px stamp on interactive elements (buttons, hamburger, skip link, focus outline). The form language is line and frame: 1px hairlines for structure; 2px gold gradient bars as accents (76px hero bar, 74px head-rule); hairline offset frames that sit askew of the photograph they frame (the hero photo's inset frame; the portrait's `::before` shifted -16px up-left and +16px past the bottom-right); a 1px dashed Gold Rule underline marking placeholder slots. Ornament is minimal authored inline SVG in gold only: the 8px rotated-square diamond separating marquee items, the three-line hamburger, and the select chevron. The wordmark is text only — no monogram, no logo mark. Photographs are always rectangular, never rounded, never masked.

## Components

### Buttons
Stamped, uppercase, deliberate.
- **Shape:** near-square (2px radius); Inter 600, 0.8rem, 0.18em tracking, uppercase; `padding: 1.05rem 2.1rem` (header variant `.7rem 1.4rem`; full-width in the hero at ≤520px).
- **Gold (primary):** the two-gold vertical gradient with a Gold Light border, Pressed Ink text. One per view region: "Book a Call" / "Check Availability" / "Send It".
- **Hairline (secondary):** transparent with a Gold Rule border, Gold Light text; hover fills faintly (`rgba(185,138,47,.07)`), brightens the border, resolves text to ink.
- **Hover:** both lift `-2px` over .35s on the house ease; gold adds the hover-lift shadow.
- **Focus:** global `:focus-visible` — 2px Gold outline, 3px offset.

### Engraved Link
The tertiary action: small-caps text (Inter 600, 0.78rem, 0.2em, uppercase, Gold Light) on a resting Gold Rule hairline; on hover a Gold Light line draws over it left-to-right (.5s, house ease) while the text resolves to ink. Used inside path rows.

### Gold Bar
The section punctuation: a 2px horizontal bar in the 90° two-gold gradient. 76px wide above the hero headline (2.4rem gap below it); 74px wide as the head-rule closing each section head (1.9rem below the lede). Never longer, never thicker, never vertical.

### Path Row (editorial row)
The How-I-Help unit: a full-width 5fr/7fr row — Playfair title, italic gold promise, engraved link on the left; a bullet-free two-column list on the right (`columns: 2`, 2.6rem column gap, .55rem row padding, grey at .97rem with ink-600 lead phrases, `break-inside: avoid`). Rows are separated by Ink Hairline rules (top rule on the group), bleed 1.5rem past the container, and fill with Panel on hover (.45s ease). No borders, no shadows, no icons. Stacks to one column and one list column at ≤900px.

### Credential Marquee
The trust strip: a full-bleed band bounded by Gold Rule Dim hairlines with a navy wash (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(11,26,51,.28), rgba(11,26,51,.08))`), 1.55rem vertical padding. Inside, credentials in the marquee voice (Playfair 700, 1.02rem, .14em, uppercase, ink) separated by 8px gold diamonds (2.6rem side margins). Two duplicate sequences (`role="list"` on the first; the clone `aria-hidden="true"`) translate -50% over a 36s linear infinite loop; hover or focus-within pauses it. At ≤900px or `pointer: coarse` the animation is removed, the clone hidden, and the first sequence wraps centered at 0.84rem (1rem diamond margins, last diamond hidden) — auto-motion exists only where hover-pause does.

### Split Hero Photo
The photo-forward signature: the portrait absolutely positioned right at 47% width (52% at ≤1100px), `object-fit: cover` from the top, graded `saturate(.82) contrast(1.05) brightness(.92)`, and blended into the ground by layered scrims — a 90° gradient from solid Near-Black at the copy edge (`.55 → .08 → .25` across), a 180° gradient sealing top and bottom, and a navy tint at .3. Over it, the hairline offset frame (Gold Rule Dim, inset 9%/12%/8%). At ≤900px the photo goes full-width behind bottom-aligned copy with a vertical scrim and no frame.

### Offset Portrait
The about treatment: a bare photograph (no mat) with the portrait-weight black shadow, a Gold Rule hairline frame offset behind it (-16px top/left, +16px right/bottom, z-index under the image), and a navy corner tint on top (`linear-gradient(200deg, rgba(11,26,51,.18), transparent 45%)`). Small-caps grey caption 1.4rem below. Caps at 460px centered when stacked.

### Photo Mosaic
Three candids in a 7fr/5fr × 2-row grid: the first figure spans both rows (min-height 520px; others 250px). Images cover-crop (`object-position: center 25%`; tall figure 15%), run `saturate(.88)`, and zoom subtly on hover (`scale(1.035)` over 1.2s). Captions are overlaid at the bottom of each figure on a caption gradient (`transparent → rgba(8,9,12,.88)`, 2.2rem top fade) in ink small caps (.74rem/.2em, 600). Stacks to one column at ≤900px (tall figure 420px, others 280px; tall drops to 340px at ≤520px).

### Contact Band
The lead-capture close: a full-bleed band (Gold Rule Dim top rule, `linear-gradient(180deg, Panel, rgba(11,26,51,.3) 130%)` fill, `clamp(5rem, 9vw, 7.5rem)` padding) holding a 5fr/7fr grid — intro left (headline with italic turn, grey lede, the reach list, italic reassurance note), form right.

### Contact Field
Underline-only fields: transparent, borderless except a 1px Ink Underline bottom rule, no radius, ink text at 1rem, grey placeholders (.75 alpha). Small-caps gold labels above (.72rem/.2em), gold-light asterisk for required. Focus turns the underline Gold (.3s) plus the global gold outline at 4px offset. Selects: `appearance: none` with an inline gold chevron SVG, options on Panel. Textareas resize vertically from 6.2rem. Field rows are 2×1fr (1.8rem gap), stacking at ≤520px. The submit row pairs the gold button with an italic grey reassurance; success swaps the form for a centered italic-gold serif line (1.9rem) + grey copy (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`, focus moved). A visually-hidden honeypot guards the form.

### Reach List
Direct-contact rows ruled by Ink Hairline (1.35rem padding, top rule on the first): a small-caps gold label above each entry; live links in ink (1.02rem, hover to Gold Light, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`); unresolved entries as placeholder slots — italic grey (.95rem) on a 1px dashed Gold Rule underline, each with an HTML `REPLACE:` comment showing the exact swap-in markup. Honest slots, never fake content.

### Navigation
Fixed header: text-only Playfair wordmark (700, 1.12rem, .22em, uppercase; hover to Gold Light) left; small-caps nav links (grey → Gold Light, .3s) and the compressed gold button right. Transparent at top; blurred near-black material after 24px of scroll. Mobile: hairline-bordered hamburger morphing to an X (aria-expanded driven); full-screen near-black overlay (.98) with Playfair 700 links at 1.7rem, closing on link tap or Escape, body scroll locked. A gold skip-link surfaces on focus. Footer repeats the text wordmark (.95rem), small-caps place line, nav links, and an Ink Hairline legal rule.

### Motion Grammar
One ease everywhere: `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`. The hero entrance: the photo breathes in via `photoIn` (opacity 0 + `scale(1.04)` → rest, 1.4s, .2s delay) while bar+headline, sub, CTAs, and meta line rise 22px on a stagger (`rise`, .9s each, delays .25/.4/.58/.76s) — all inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. The marquee loops 36s linear, pausing on hover/focus-within, static on touch/narrow. Below the fold, `.reveal` elements (visible by default; JS adds the hidden state via `html.js`) fade up 18px over .8s via IntersectionObserver (`rootMargin: 0 0 -8%`, threshold .1, unobserve after firing) with `data-d="1|2|3"` sibling stagger at .08s steps. Micro-motion: -2px button lifts (.35s), path-row hover fill (.45s), mosaic zoom (1.2s), drawn link underlines (.5s), .3–.4s color/border shifts, hamburger-to-X morph. Reduced motion collapses every animation and transition to ~0ms, stops the marquee, and disables smooth scroll; no-JS shows everything.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** blend every photograph into the ground: desaturate (`saturate(.82–.88)`), scrim with the near-black/navy gradient family, and let at least one edge dissolve to `#08090c` or carry an overlaid caption gradient.
- **Do** draw structure with rules — Ink Hairline for row separators, Gold Rule Dim for band edges and quiet frames, Gold Rule for emphatic frames — and punctuate sections with the 2px two-gold bar (74–76px).
- **Do** end headlines with an italic Playfair turn in Gold Light, and reserve `<strong>`-in-grey (ink, 600) as the only in-paragraph emphasis.
- **Do** spend gold on at most one gold button per view region, pairing it with a hairline button or engraved link for the quieter path.
- **Do** keep motion on the single house ease, opt-in via JS (`html.js`), gated by `prefers-reduced-motion` — and give any auto-playing motion a hover/focus pause plus a static fallback on touch and narrow screens.
- **Do** mark unresolved facts as labeled placeholder slots (italic grey on dashed gold underline) with `REPLACE:` comments — honest slots, never invented content.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gradient text. Gradients live only on surfaces (button face, 2px bars); type is always solid ink or gold.
- **Don't** build icon-card grids or boxed section cards. The only icons are the gold diamond, hamburger, and select chevron; repeatable content is hairline-ruled editorial rows.
- **Don't** put a kicker or small-caps eyebrow above any heading. The small-caps voice is content (meta lines, captions, labels), never a heading's warm-up.
- **Don't** cast colored or glowing shadows. Shadows are soft black and downward; overlays stay in the near-black/navy family.
- **Don't** round corners past the 2px stamp radius, and never on photos, frames, rows, or fields.
- **Don't** introduce new hues or bright unscrimmed imagery. The world is a near-black ground, two panels, two inks, two golds, and a navy that lives only inside gradients.
