You’re not failing. You’re playing the wrong game.

Coach Burt’s whole premise is one sentence long: you’re not under-skilled. You’re over-committed to a game you’ve already outgrown. See if any of this sounds familiar.

You’re successful, and underutilized. The ceiling isn’t your effort. It’s the room.

You’ve outgrown your role, your market, maybe your industry, and you can feel it.

You want better returns on the same hours, not longer hours.

You know your skill deserves more income and more impact than the current game pays out.

If you nodded at any of those, hear this: frustration isn’t a warning sign. It’s a signal, and reading it correctly is exactly what The Big Table teaches.

Two hours. One formula. Your next move.

This isn’t a hype session. Coach Burt walks the room through The Big Table Formula, the same framework he uses with the high performers he coaches:

  • Spot the moment you’ve outgrown your game The signs are predictable once you know them. Most people read them as burnout and slow down. Exactly backwards.
  • Read frustration as a signal, not a failure The thing gnawing at you isn’t weakness. It’s data about the size of the table you’re sitting at.
  • Name your primary value-creating skill Get ruthlessly clear on the one thing you do that creates most of your value, and stop diluting it.
  • Choose bigger problems and better vehicles Same talent, pointed at a bigger problem in a better vehicle, pays completely differently.
  • Think in leverage, reputation, and positioning How the people at bigger tables actually think, so you stop trading hours and start compounding.
  • Then the table talk starts You’ll be shoulder to shoulder with owners, agents, and operators from across North Mississippi. The room is half the value.
