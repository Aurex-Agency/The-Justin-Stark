Do right by people. The rest follows.

I’m Justin Stark, and I own Pontotoc Insurance Agency in Pontotoc, Mississippi. Most days you’ll find me across the table from a family working out the questions that keep them up at night: Can we actually retire? Will the money last? What happens to everything we built if something happens to me?

My answer has never changed: you deserve to understand your own plan. Not a binder of fine print. Not a pitch. A straight explanation of where you stand, what your options really cost, and a strategy you could describe to your kids over Sunday dinner.

The other half of my work is people who do what I do. I build agents, through contracting, coaching, and systems, because I believe this industry gets better when the people in it are taught to do right by folks first and let the business follow. That’s also what I talk about when someone hands me a microphone.
