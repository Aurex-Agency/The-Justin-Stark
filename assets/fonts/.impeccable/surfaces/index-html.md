# Surface brief — index.html (thejustinstark.com)

Scope: the entire site — one page. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience & job: mixed visitors (families/pre-retirees, agents, event hosts) arriving from a conference or referral, mostly on phones; each must self-select a path and reach out. Primary action: Book a Call / submit the lead form. Secondary: email or tap-to-call.

Proof on hand: real photography only (studio headshot, MedicareCon stage shots, office candids in `assets/img/`). No testimonials, stats, or press — credential strip stays role-descriptors; nothing fabricated.

Chosen direction (seed 6cd3e333, own grounded candidate 7, brief-pinned palette/type): **engraved Southern invitation suite** — the page as a formal invitation die-stamped in champagne gold on near-black card stock; sections are the cards of the suite laid on a table; the lead form is the RSVP reply card. Playfair Display display voice (700–900 + italic), Inter functional voice. Hairline double rules, monogram seal, ledger-ruled lists instead of icon cards. Memorable moment: the hero border draws itself like a die-stamp on arrival; the form is a reply card that thanks you in engraved italic.

Constraints: single static file + assets; Formspree/scheduler/phone/conference-name ship as commented swap points and honest placeholder slots; smooth-scroll nav (#work #about #speaking #contact); hamburger <900px; reduced-motion safe; no kickers, no gradient text, no icon-card grids.

Unresolved: phone number, scheduler URL, conference name, form endpoint, og-image/favicon final art (favicon ships as authored JS-monogram SVG).
