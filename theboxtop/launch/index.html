The box top problem

You have the pieces. Do you have the picture?

You ever try to put together a thousand-piece puzzle without the picture on the box? That's how most folks feel about retirement.

You've got the 401(k), the savings, the old policy from twenty years ago, and advice from everybody and their uncle. You've done a lot right. But nobody's ever shown you how it all fits together.

Clarity precedes confidence.

You can't feel good about a decision when you can't see the picture. The Box Top Method gives you the picture on the box first, so every move after that makes sense.
